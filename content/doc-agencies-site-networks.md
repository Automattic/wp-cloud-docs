# WP Cloud for agencies and site networks

WP Cloud is an API-first platform for organizations building a WordPress
hosting product. Direct integration requires a partner to provide customer
onboarding, a management interface, billing, documentation, and frontline
support. It does not include an agency-management dashboard by default.

For most agencies and companies with a modest site portfolio, a WP Cloud-powered
host such as [Pressable](https://pressable.com/) or [Bluehost
Cloud](https://www.bluehost.com/hosting/cloud) provides the platform benefits
without the engineering and operational investment of a direct integration.

Direct WP Cloud partnership is generally a better fit for an organization that
manages hundreds of sites, expects continued growth, and intends to make
hosting a substantial part of its service. A growing agency can still be a fit
when it has a clear hosting-product strategy and the engineering and support
capacity to operate it.

Review [Key onboarding steps](/docs/getting-started/onboard-launch/) and
the [WP Cloud API quick start](/docs/api-automation/api-quick-start/) when
evaluating that investment.
