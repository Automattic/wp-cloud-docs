# Copyright infringement and takedown notifications

When WP Cloud or Automattic receives a copyright-infringement notice concerning
a site operated by a WP Cloud host partner, WP Cloud forwards the notice to the
partner's designated contact. The partner is responsible for reviewing the
notice and acting under its WP Cloud agreement, its own policies, and applicable
law. Automattic's public [Copyright
Policy](https://automattic.com/copyright-policy/) provides additional context.

This page summarizes the WP Cloud notification workflow. It is not legal advice,
and the governing agreement and notice control when they differ from this
summary.

## Respond within 48 hours

WP Cloud requires a host partner to take one of these actions within 48
hours of receiving a DMCA or other takedown notice:

1. Remove the allegedly infringing content; or
2. determine that it is not infringing, notify WP Cloud of that determination,
   and accept responsibility for keeping it published.

When the content is removed, no reply is normally required unless the notice
asks for confirmation. To dispute a claim, reply to the notification with the
specific basis, supporting documents, and the required acceptance of liability.
The partner assumes responsibility for damages, expenses, and legal fees that
result from continuing to publish disputed content under the terms described
in its agreement.

Consult qualified legal counsel before disputing a notice. Fair use, a valid
license, permission from the rightsholder, and public-domain status can be
relevant, but WP Cloud does not decide those questions for the partner.

If the partner cannot meet the deadline, reply to the notice immediately. When
no action is taken within 48 hours, Automattic can act directly to maintain
compliance and the failure can affect the partnership.

## Notification delivery

Notifications use a subject such as `[Action Required] WP Cloud Copyright/DMCA
Notification` and are sent to the partner's designated email contacts. Keep
those contacts current, monitor the mailbox, and route the subject to the team
authorized to remove or dispute content.

Do not send a new general support request for an active notice. Reply in the
existing notification thread so the response stays attached to the correct
site, claimant, and deadline.

## Repeat infringement

Partners must maintain and enforce an appropriate repeat-infringer policy.
Multiple valid complaints about the same end user can require additional
preventive measures or termination. [17 U.S.C. §
512(i)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512) describes the
repeat-infringer condition for United States safe-harbor eligibility; partners
should obtain legal advice on the policy that applies to their service and
jurisdictions.
